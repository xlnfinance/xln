## Priority fixes by reviewer

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · mobile-light · 790

The mobile light theme demonstrates exceptional clarity and rigorous architectural intent with its one-scale visceral bars and instant provable state cues. However, it suffers from crypto-native visual clutter (raw 64-character hashes, uncurated metadata chips) and recurring frontend rendering artifacts like stray blue ticks and bottom-tab scroll overlaps.

- Fix viewport scroll container padding across all screens (`padding-bottom: 96px`) to prevent bottom tab bars and floating sheets from truncating actions and metadata.
- Eliminate the stray absolute blue tick/pseudo-element rendering bug present on the left margins of Activity, Receipt, and Pay views.
- Truncate all raw 42/64-character hexadecimal IDs (destination addresses, runtime IDs, proofs) to middle-ellipses (`0x1234...abcd`) with native copy interactions.
- Unify visceral value bars on zero/small balances with a permanent 1px background track so small payments ($25) do not render as invisible 0.5px slivers.
- Fix disabled button color contrast on Swap and secondary action buttons to meet minimum WCAG AA 4.5:1 legibility standards in light mode.

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · desktop-light · 835

The desktop light theme successfully translates xln's core visceral value model into a remarkably clean, disciplined fintech aesthetic with legible bilateral bars and convincing provability cues. However, it suffers from tablet-like center clustering with excessive empty right-rail canvas, stray rendering artifacts on active states, and inconsistent container treatments across subpages.

- Fix visual rendering artifacts across desktop views: remove stray orphaned blue dots on active activity rows and modal backdrop edges.
- Anchor the desktop grid: establish a disciplined 1120px max-width container with a balanced 7fr / 5fr two-column layout to prevent floating cards and deserted screen edges.
- Clean up QR code surface on Receive: eliminate the off-color beige glow (#FBF9F1) in favor of a crisp bordered white card consistent with the rest of the light system.
- Unify container architecture: place loose settings rows (Runtime/Vault), empty lane triggers, and legends inside standard 16px rounded bordered white cards instead of raw text on canvas.
- Refine micro-bar rendering: enforce a minimum 3px visible threshold with anchor tick marks so sub-scale values (like $25 at 1px=$50) do not look like dead pixel noise.

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · desktop-dark · 816

The visceral value bar model and dark fintech aesthetic establish an exceptionally strong, provable design foundation. However, desktop viewports suffer from severe lower-canvas abandonment, creating a stretched mobile app feel rather than an authoritative two-column workstation.

- Fix desktop space utilization: replace cavernous empty space on Home, Swap, Account, and Activity with ledger history, order depths, or framed cards in a balanced max-w-6xl grid.
- Fix sub-$50 bar visibility: establish a minimum 3px rounded pill width for small bar values at 1px=$50 scale so transactions like $25 do not appear as 1px rendering glitches.
- Anchor the scale legend and bilateral bars: integrate color keys directly into bar modules across Home, Account, and Settings rather than detaching them as distant text legends.
- Eliminate raw hex sprawl: consistently truncate 32-byte hashes and 20-byte addresses across Pay, Settings, and Activity with dedicated copy-on-click badges.
- Dark-theme QR code styling: soften the harsh stark-white QR container on Receive with a rounded card border and subtle background matching the dark container hierarchy.

### openrouter/google/gemini-flash-latest · mobile-dark · 839

xln mobile dark delivers an impressively focused, high-trust fintech aesthetic with disciplined typography and genuine visual rigor around its visceral scale concept. It is held back from top-tier polish by stray pixel artifacts, raw multi-line hexadecimal hashes, and scroll containers that clip harshly against modal sheets and the bottom tab bar.

- Fix floating scale bar artifacts: eliminate the orphan 2px blue ticks on left screen edges (seen in Activity, Receipt, and Pay) and ensure small amounts have a minimum 4px thumb width on a subtle track.
- Add explicit zero-anchor notches: insert a crisp 2px tick (#475569) at the zero balance divider of bilateral account bars across Home, Account, and Pay screens.
- Format cryptographic primitives: truncate raw 66-character hashes and Entity IDs to 10-character ellipses with tap-to-copy badges across Pay, Receive, and Settings.
- Fix viewport scroll insets: enforce 96px bottom padding across Home, Account, and Settings to prevent cards and action links from colliding with the floating bottom tab bar.
- Relocate scale ruler preview: move the 'At this scale' visual comparison card directly under the 'Dollars per pixel' slider on Settings instead of burying it at the bottom.

### openrouter/x-ai/grok-latest · desktop-light · 690

Light desktop is a calm two-column shell with a real bilateral bar and strong Pay/Account trust copy, but it is not yet a shipping fintech surface: money bars do not share one pixel scale, Home and Activity drown the total in legends and chips, and Swap/Receipt/Receive fail the visceral-value brief. Light theme contrast is acceptable, yet chrome (pills, hub chips, triple labels) keeps it from feeling expensive.

- Enforce one absolute bar scale (1 px = $N) on Home total, token row, Hub One, Pay debit, Activity ±amounts, and Receive capacity; fade overflow, never restack widths per card.
- Home: delete the On-chain/Reserve/Accounts dollar legend and Instant send/receive duplicate; one stacked bar under the total, bilateral numbers only on the account bar.
- Collapse Pay/Receive/Swap (and Account’s three pills) to one primary action; treat the rest as ghost/text.
- Receipt: human time, copyable proof, a $25-scale bar; remove 1970 ms / 02:00:48 debug.
- Swap: never present 0.00 receive + dead Swap; quote or explicit empty, and hatch the 100 USDC hold on the Hub One bar.
