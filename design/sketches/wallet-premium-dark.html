xln wallet, four screens

Phone and desktop rendered from the same components and the same demo data. Every bar on this page shares one scale. Drag the slider: a dollar is one pixel, or ten, or a hundred. Long money runs off the screen until you zoom out. That is the point.

Scale 1 px = $10 Applies to every bar in every frame below. In the product this lives in Settings.

Visceral value

A bar is exactly as long as the money is. No percentages, no per-row normalisation. Two rows are comparable at a glance because they are drawn to the same rule.

Three places money lives

On-chain wallet (yours, slow), reserve in the Depository (escrowed, enforceable), bilateral accounts (instant, credit-backed). Folded by default into one number per token and one total.

Color is a money state

Never decoration. Slate is on-chain, mint is collateral and settled, indigo is credit and instant, amber is pending or owed, violet is in flight, red is dispute.

One code, every screen

Under 840 px: tab bar and bottom sheets. Above: icon rail and two columns. Same components, same data, same scale. Native shells only wrap it.
